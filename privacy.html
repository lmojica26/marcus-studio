Marcus Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

  1. What we access. With the account owner's permission, the app receives from TikTok: a basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar) and permission to upload videos to that account's drafts (user.info.basic, video.upload).
  2. How it is used. Only to confirm which account is connected and to upload the owner's own videos to that account's drafts.
  3. Storage. Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer only. No data is sold, shared, or sent to third parties, and no data about other TikTok users is collected.
  4. Deletion. Revoke access in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions); the locally stored token is then deleted on request.
  5. Google data. The companion tool "Reacher" (same owner) uses Google APIs (Sheets, Drive, Docs, Calendar, Gmail, Contacts) only for the owner's own account, to keep the owner's production spreadsheet and files up to date. Google user data is not sold, shared, transferred to third parties, or used for advertising or to train AI models. Reacher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
